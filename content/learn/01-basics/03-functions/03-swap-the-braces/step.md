---
title: Swap the braces for =
---

Line 1 is the same `area` as before, with `=` in place of the braces.
Press Run.

A body that is a single expression can follow `=`.
Line 1 means exactly `area` with braces around `width * height`, so its last semicolon ends the declaration and throws nothing away.
In this form the return type must still be written, so you can see what `area` gives back without reading its body.
