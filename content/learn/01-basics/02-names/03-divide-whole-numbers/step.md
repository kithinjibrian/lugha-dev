---
title: Divide whole numbers
---

`total` on line 2 is an `i64`, a whole number.
Before you run it, decide what line 3, `total / 2`, prints.
Then press Run.

It prints `3`.
Both sides of `/` are `i64`, so the result is an `i64` too.
Line 4 divides two `f64`s, numbers with a decimal point, and prints `3.5`.
