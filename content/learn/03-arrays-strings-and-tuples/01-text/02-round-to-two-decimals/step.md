---
title: Round to two decimals
---

Line 3 prints `share` the way `println` would.
Line 4 adds `:.2` after the name, which asks for two decimals.
Before you run it, decide whether `.2` rounds `0.666…` or cuts it off.
Then press Run.

It rounds, to `0.67`.
A precision like `.2` works only on floats.
