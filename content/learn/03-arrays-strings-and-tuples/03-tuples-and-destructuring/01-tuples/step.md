title: Tuples
---

A tuple groups a fixed number of values, each with its own type.
Line 2 makes one from a string and a number, in parentheses.
Its elements are read by position, with `.0` and `.1` on lines 4 and 5.
Inside the tuple the string prints with its quotes, so will `item.0` on line 4 print them too?
Press Run.

It prints `milk` without them: the quotes show when a string is printed inside a tuple or an array, not on its own.
