---
title: Skip a turn with continue, stop with break
---

Both loops can be cut short from inside.
`continue` skips the rest of the block and goes on to the next turn, and `break` ends the loop.
Here line 4 skips every multiple of 3, and line 7 ends the loop once `i` is past 7, though the range goes up to 9.
Run it and see where it stops.
