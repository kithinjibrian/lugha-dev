---
title: Iterators
---

Besides arrays, `for … of` steps through iterators, values that give back one value at a time.
`range(1, 4)` on line 2 gives `1` to `3`, and `chars()` on line 5 gives a string's characters.
`iter()` on line 8 makes an iterator over an array, from its own copy of the array.
Press Run.
