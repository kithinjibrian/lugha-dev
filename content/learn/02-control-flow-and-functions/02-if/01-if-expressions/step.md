---
title: if expressions
---

Like a block, an `if` has a value, the value of whichever branch ran.
Here lines 3 to 9 put one of three strings into `advice`, and `else if` on line 5 adds a second condition to check.
Press Run.

It prints `stay inside`, because `temperature > 30` is true and the other branches are skipped.
Change `31` on line 2 to `25` or `10` and run again to see the other two.
