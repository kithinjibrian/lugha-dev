title: Struct declaration
---

A struct is a type you declare yourself, with a name for each of its fields.
Line 1 declares `Point`, a struct with two fields, each with its type.
`Point` sits outside `main`, because a struct is declared at the top level, and it's a type like `i64`.
Line 4 builds one, naming each field, and `p.x` on line 5 reads the field `x`.
Press Run.
